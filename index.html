Controlled product architecture

One platform. Four coordinated workstreams.

The program is structured to keep product design, intellectual property, regulatory strategy, and evidence generation aligned from the beginning.

01

Formulation

Define composition, stability, aerosol performance, analytical methods, and controlled manufacturing requirements.

02

Device

Develop a capsule-based dry-powder inhaler interface, airflow pathway, user needs, verification, and human-factors plan.

03

Packaging

Protect dose integrity through moisture control, storage, handling, labeling, traceability, and transport requirements.

04

Evidence

Build the nonclinical, clinical, quality, and regulatory record required to support responsible U.S. development.

U.S. development roadmap

Move through decisions—not assumptions.

Each stage is a decision gate. Work advances only when ownership, technical evidence, physician input, risk controls, and regulatory strategy support the next investment.

  1. 01

    Clear

    Name, entity, inventorship, ownership, background IP, and disclosure status.

  2. 02

    Protect

    Confidentiality, assignments, patentability search, freedom-to-operate scoping, and filing preparation.

  3. 03

    Define

    Product description, user needs, requirements, design inputs, formulation hypotheses, and testing plans.

  4. 04

    Align

    Product classification, Pre-RFD, pre-IND questions, quality strategy, and FDA interaction planning.

  5. 05

    Test

    Formulation, aerosol, device, stability, usability, nonclinical, and clinical feasibility.

  6. 06

    Decide

    Go/no-go, licensing, partners, financing, filing, IND-enabling work, and clinical execution.
