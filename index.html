Why NAD+

A biological foundation. A product-specific development question.

NAD+ participates in cellular energy metabolism, DNA-repair pathways, and cell signaling. Those biological roles do not, by themselves, establish that an inhaled VYTAL AIR product is safe or effective. Product-specific proof must be developed.

How It Works

Science and development library

From biological rationale to product-specific proof.

The science program is divided into evidence layers so general pulmonary-delivery literature is not confused with validation of the final VYTAL AIR product.

Layer 1

NAD+ Biology

Redox reactions, energy metabolism, signaling, sirtuin biology, and DNA-repair pathways.

General biological literature—not product efficacy.
Layer 2

Pulmonary Delivery

Lung physiology, absorption surfaces, clearance, local toxicity, systemic delivery, and route constraints.

Platform literature—not VYTAL AIR validation.
Layer 3

Particle Engineering

Aerodynamic diameter, morphology, moisture sensitivity, dispersibility, emitted dose, and fine-particle fraction.

Requires VYTAL AIR laboratory data.
Layer 4

DPI Device Science

Flow resistance, capsule opening, powder retention, deagglomeration, technique, cleaning, and durability.

Requires verified design and test reports.
Layer 5

Product-Specific Proof

CMC, inhalation toxicology, local tolerability, pharmacokinetics, safety, device, and clinical evidence.

Development program not yet completed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is established—and what is not.

What is VYTAL AIR?

VYTAL AIR is a development-stage VYTAL House program evaluating a capsule-based dry-powder inhalation platform. It is not currently presented as an FDA-approved commercial product.

Is the final formulation established?

No. The formulation, excipients, dose, capsule, particle attributes, manufacturing process, analytical controls, stability profile, and device compatibility require qualified development and testing.

Is VYTAL AIR patent pending?

The phrase “patent pending” should only be used after an actual patent application is filed and the filing receipt is retained. This website does not represent that filing as completed unless separately confirmed.

Does VYTAL AIR require FDA review?

The proposed U.S. program is being planned for FDA classification and development review. FDA determines the final center, pathway, study requirements, labeling, and authorization.

Can people purchase or use it now?

No consumer-sales or self-use authorization is represented here. Interest registration is for updates, professional review, research planning, and development partnerships.

How can a physician, scientist, engineer, or manufacturer participate?

Contact VYTAL House and identify your discipline, relevant inhalation or combination-product experience, geography, capacity, and proposed contribution. Confidential technical discussions should begin only after an appropriate NDA.
